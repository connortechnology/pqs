Credit, Billing and Production Policies

First-time and non-credit account orders may be subject to a production down payment with the balance to be paid in full before the project leaves our production facilities. Available non-credit account payment options may include Cash, Certified Cheque, Visa, Mastercard or Bank Draft. Clients requiring credit may apply via our on-line credit application. Terms may be available to clients upon approved credit.

Production specifications and prices are subject to the viewing of artwork, film or electronic file. Please verify all project specifications listed in the project docket for accuracy. All projects are to be fulfilled as per the specifications listed in the project docket(s) unless otherwise documented in writing prior to production. Quantities of +/- 10% will constitute completion of order, overs and unders may be charged or credited appropriately. All project files and proofs must be approved prior to production. Heavy ink coverage, non-standard production files or additional services required may be subject to additional charges. will in no way be liable for incidental or consequential damages in excess of the project purchase price.

Re-Ordering Projects

At anytime you may create an re-order for any project contained in this quote by clicking on any "Project ID" above. After following the link and logging into our web site with your email address and password, click on the "Add to Quote" or "Add to Order" on the the "Project View Current Project" page. If 30 days have elapsed since the project was original created you will have to click the "Update Price" button before continuing. You will receive an instantaneous copy via email upon the the completion of the quote or order process. For your convenience all projects, quotes and orders will be stored in your own private account history for future modifications or reorders.
