Credit, Billing and Production Policies

First-time and non-credit account orders may be subject to a production down payment with the balance to be paid in full before the project leaves our production facilities. Available non-credit account payment options may include Cash, Certified Cheque, Visa, Mastercard or Bank Draft. Clients requiring credit may apply via our on-line credit application. Terms may be available to clients upon approved credit.

All CTP quotes must include a digital proof. Production specifications and prices quoted are subject to the viewing of artwork, film or electronic file. Please verify all project specifications listed in the project docket(s) for accuracy. If project specifications or prices appear to be questionable please contact us via our helpdesk or call: before ordering or preparing reseller quotations. Quoted prices may be subject to paper price increases, availability, applicable taxes and are valid for 30 days.
