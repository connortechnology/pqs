What are the advantages of registration?

Registration allows customers to track orders, get quote online, reorder from order history, get automated reminders for file/artwork upload and order delivery.

What is custom print product?

Custom print order means you design, upload artwork, place an order and we print. The access to place an order and review prices are available only to registered customers. Custom printing is available for most common print products; Wall Calendars, Flyers, Letterhead, Envelope, Carbonless NCR Forms, Door Hanger, Magnets and Presentation Folders. Online custom print order allows registered customers to select; from standard sizes, number of pages, colours, paper stock, and finishing.

What is standard print product?

Standard print products are pre-designed and ready to print. A registered customer can oder a standard product online. This product does not require any design/layout or uploading artwork. The products are designed by us and are ready to order. Our design allows customers to make it unique and their own by inserting a business card in the magnetic placeholder. Example of standard product; Magnetic Calendar. Browse our designs and download samples to know more.

What is stock print product?

Stock print products means we print customer's annual (or any desired time period) print requirement, hold it in inventory and deliver printed products as and when required by customer. Please call (905) 501-1296 to know more.

What is the difference between file upload & FTP Access?

File Upload: This is restricted to custom print products ordered via our online store. Example: If you selct and place an order for a product using online store product matrix, then you have to upload file via your online order system and this refered to as file upload. These uploads are unique to each online order placed. FTP access is available for direct orders (orders not placed via our online store).
