Quotes » Instructions

Step 1: Select a Product or Create a Custom Project
To create a project with predefined specifications, click on any of the product pages listed in the product menu above and select a product category. Once on the product category of choice, you may select your product of choice and click on the "Save and Contune" button.

To create a project with your own custom specifications click on the "Create Project" link from the project menu above or to the left of the screen.

Step 2: Project Type and Services
Select the type of printing required, project and creation mode (Custom Projects Only), request up to three different quantities, design services, packaging or fulfilment options before inputting a reference name for the project and clicking on the "Save & Continue" button.

Step 3: View Service Details (Custom Projects Only)
Selet your own custom project specifications and the individual service pages will automatically calculate prices. When finished click "Save & Continue" to add the service to your project. Our intelligent estimation process automatically considers all possible production equipment, run styles, stock sizes, impositions and materials before calculating the most cost-effective way to produce the highest quality products.

Step 4: Confirm All Services
Confirm all services and prices in your project and click "Add to Quote" or "Add to Order".

Step 5: Markup Prices (Reseller Option Only )
End users will automatically skip this step, however if you have been set-up with a reseller account, you will have the ability to add an additional markups to your quote prices (Leave Blank for No Markup) before preparing copies of the quoation to be automatically sent on your behalf to your own customer.

Step 6: Quote Information
Input personal and your customer (Reseller Option Only) contact information to enable a copy of the quotation to be sent via e-mail to yourself and someone else on your behalf.

Step 7: Submit Quote
Confirm quote information and submit the quote. A copy of the quote will be automatically sent to the appropriate email addresses and stored in your own private account history.

* Note: For your convenience all projects, quotes and orders will be stored in your own private account history for future modifications or reorders.
