Certain projects can benefit from having a specific grain directions. Business cards are best printed with the grain running along their width for a stiffer card. Perfect bound books are best with the grain parallel to the spine (height) to prevent paper distortion due to glueing. Please choose a specific direction or choose ”No Preference„ if the grain direction isn’t a factor.
