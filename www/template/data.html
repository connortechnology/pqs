Data Set

Datasets may be uploaded for bulk setting or downloaded for editing or archival purposes.

Upload

For large datasets, bulk uploading of information may be preferable over inputing individual records. Files must be provided in CSV format with the first line consisting of the header with field names matching that of the template.

Download

You can download your currently entered dataset in common formats; for editing in any text based editor or spreadsheet application (Microsoft Excel™, Gnumeric, etc.).

Formats: CSV | TSV
